## How do I test without merchant accounts?

eSewa and Fonepay have public sandbox credentials, and Khalti gives you a test key at test-admin.khalti.com. connectIPS, CyberSource, NPS, IME Pay and NEPALPAY QR need credentials from the provider or your bank. [Testing and go-live](/docs/guides/testing/) lists every sandbox value and test login.

## A provider changed its API. What now?

Open an issue or pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/broisnees/nepal-payments-skill) with the source: an official doc, an SDK commit or a captured sandbox response. New adapters need the provider's published test vectors as tests before the implementation.
